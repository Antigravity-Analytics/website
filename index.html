Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026Website privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit this website or contact Antigravity Analytics Private Limited.

1. Who is responsible

Antigravity Analytics Private Limited is responsible for the information it receives through this website and through messages sent to its published email addresses.

2. Information involved

  • Technical delivery data: IP address, browser and device details, request time, requested file, referring page and basic security logs may be processed by the site host to deliver and protect the website.
  • Correspondence: if you email us, we receive the address you use, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Website preferences: this build does not create a first-party account, profile or persistent preference record.

3. Why information is used

Technical data is used to deliver the site, maintain security, diagnose faults and understand aggregate demand. Correspondence is used to answer the purpose of your message, evaluate a partnership or investment conversation, and keep a necessary business record.

4. Analytics programme

Antigravity uses Simple Analytics to understand aggregate page views, broad traffic sources and anonymous interaction events (section views, button and link clicks). The purpose is to improve the clarity, accessibility and reliability of the site, not to build advertising profiles. Simple Analytics sets no cookies, collects no personal data, and honours Do Not Track.

Audience analytics is active via Simple Analytics as described above. No advertising tracker is active. Planned analytics will not capture message contents, keystrokes, clipboard contents, precise location, identity documents, sensitive attributes or screen recordings.

5. Sharing

Information may be handled by infrastructure and email providers acting for Antigravity, or disclosed where required by law. It is not sold as a personal-data list. We do not use this website to collect payment-card details, government identifiers or sensitive profile information.

6. Retention

Hosting security records are retained according to the host’s operational schedule. Email correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the conversation, legal obligations and legitimate company records. A specific deletion request may be limited where retention is legally required.

7. Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain uses of personal information. You may also withdraw consent where consent is the basis of processing.

8. Contact

For a privacy question or request, write to hello@antigrav.si. Do not send identity documents unless we specifically confirm they are necessary and provide a secure route.

Cookie & analytics policy

Last updated 10 October 2026Version 1.1

This policy explains every category of browser storage and measurement relevant to antigrav.si and what is active now.

Current status: Antigravity uses Simple Analytics, a privacy-first analytics service that sets no cookies and collects no personal data. No advertising tracker is active. The notice can be dismissed for this visit, but the choice is not written to a cookie or browser database and may appear again after a reload.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text records a website or service can ask a browser to retain. Related technologies include local browser storage, software-development-kit identifiers, pixels and tags. They can support essential delivery, remember preferences, measure use or build advertising profiles. This policy uses “cookies” as a convenient term for these technologies unless a distinction matters.

3. Analytics in use

Antigravity wants to understand whether the website communicates the company clearly. Simple Analytics measures aggregate page views only:

  • page views, entry pages and broad referral source;
  • coarse device, browser, language and country-level information.

That measurement would be used to improve narrative flow, reliability, accessibility and the effectiveness of company communications. It would not be used to read email contents, capture typed text, record a replay of the screen, collect precise location, or combine activity with an advertising profile.

4. Consent

Simple Analytics sets no cookies and collects no personal data, so it does not require consent under GDPR or ePrivacy rules and runs for all visitors. If Antigravity ever introduces measurement that does require consent, it will not run until an appropriate consent choice has been made, the notice will offer a clear decline option with equal prominence, and withdrawing consent will be as straightforward as giving it.

5. Retention and minimisation

Any future analytics configuration will collect the minimum fields needed for aggregate product decisions, use the shortest practical retention period, restrict access to people working on the website, and document the provider and exact duration in this table. Antigravity will avoid persistent identifiers where useful measurement can be achieved without them.

6. Third parties and international transfer

Simple Analytics and Google Fonts are the named external browser-delivered services. Their infrastructure may process requests in another country. Hosting and network providers may also route technical data internationally as part of ordinary internet delivery. Any additional analytics provider will be named here before activation.

7. Browser controls

You can block or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking all cookies can affect websites that rely on sign-in or saved preferences, although this build of antigrav.si does not require them for its core content. Privacy extensions may also restrict external font requests.

8. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Simple Analytics is configured to honour Do Not Track (data-collect-dnt), so visits from browsers sending that signal are not counted. No advertising tracker is active, so there is no advertising profile for these signals to alter.

9. First-party measurement principles

If Antigravity activates its own analytics, the system will be designed around purpose limitation. Events will be collected only to answer defined questions about whether the site is understandable, reliable and useful. The company will not collect data merely because a browser makes it technically possible. Event names, fields and retention periods will be reviewed before release and reduced when a less intrusive signal can answer the same question.

Where a consent requirement applies, analytics will remain inactive until consent is given. Declining analytics will not hide content, slow the site, disable contact information or reduce access to any public section.

10. Events a future setup could measure

None of these events is measured today. A future consent-based configuration may record an event when a visitor opens a major section, starts or pauses a film, follows a navigation control, opens a policy, copies an email address or selects an outbound email link. An event may include the event time, page version, broad device class, browser family, language, approximate country or region, referring-site category and a short-lived pseudonymous session identifier.

Antigravity does not intend to collect the text of messages, clipboard contents, keystrokes, form-field values, precise location, contact lists, installed applications, biometric information or a recording of the visitor’s screen. It will not deliberately create a fingerprint from fonts, hardware, canvas output or similar signals.

11. Information that will not be used for advertising

Analytics information collected for this site will not be used to select personalised advertisements, create a cross-site advertising audience, infer sensitive interests or sell a visitor profile. Antigravity will not ask an analytics provider to combine site activity with unrelated browsing history for ad targeting.

12. Consent records

If analytics is activated, Antigravity may need to retain a limited record showing that a consent choice was made. That record would contain the choice, the policy version, the time of the choice and a random preference identifier. It would not be used as an advertising identifier. The exact storage method and duration will be published here before use.

A visitor who withdraws consent will stop future optional measurement. Withdrawal does not automatically erase aggregate statistics already produced from earlier lawful processing, but a linked pseudonymous record will be deleted or de-identified where required and technically possible.

13. Purpose, legal basis and necessity

Essential network processing supports delivery, fraud prevention, availability and security. Optional audience analytics, if introduced, will rely on consent where consent is required by applicable law. In jurisdictions where another legal basis is available, Antigravity will still honour the controls described in the notice and will document any material difference in this policy.

14. Providers and access controls

Before any analytics provider is enabled, this policy will name the provider, describe its role, link to its relevant privacy information, identify whether it acts as a processor or independent controller, and state the configured retention period. Access to raw event data will be limited to authorised people working on this website, its security or company communications. Access will be reviewed and removed when no longer needed.

15. Aggregation, deletion and retention

Raw event-level records will be kept for the shortest practical period needed to validate collection, resolve errors and prepare aggregate reports. Longer-term reports should use totals, rates and trends rather than visitor-level histories. Deletion schedules will cover production data, routine backups and exports where technically feasible. A specific schedule will replace this general statement before optional analytics starts.

16. Security and incident response

Reasonable organisational and technical controls will be applied to analytics data, including access restriction, encryption in transit, service logging and vendor review. No internet service can promise absolute security. If an incident creates a legally reportable risk, Antigravity will follow applicable notification and remediation duties.

17. Children and sensitive information

This website is directed to business, technical, partnership and investment audiences and is not designed to profile children. Antigravity does not seek sensitive personal information through analytics. Visitors should not include medical, financial, government-identification or other sensitive information in an initial email.

18. Regional rights and complaints

Privacy rights vary by location and may include access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. A request will be assessed under the law that applies to the requester and the processing. Antigravity may ask for proportionate verification before acting, but will not request more information than necessary to verify and complete the request.

If you believe a privacy concern has not been resolved, you may also have the right to contact the privacy or data-protection authority responsible for your region.

19. Changes to this policy

The version and date above will change when the categories, providers, purposes, controls or retention periods change. Material changes will be reflected in the notice shown on the site. Earlier policy versions may be retained for governance and legal records.

20. Questions

Questions about cookies, analytics or privacy can be sent to hello@antigrav.si. Please describe the nature of the request and the country or region relevant to it, but do not send identity documents or other sensitive records unless Antigravity confirms they are necessary and provides a secure route.
