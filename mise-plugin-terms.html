1. What the plugin is

A checking tool. You supply numbers in a ChatGPT conversation — order, delivery, and bill lines; emergency purchase prices; or pack prices — and the plugin compares and calculates them with exact decimal code and shows you the result, including the numbers it used.

2. What the plugin is not

  • Not connected: it is not connected to any till, point-of-sale, accounting, supplier, or bank system, and it will never ask for a login to one.
