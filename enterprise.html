Mise for enterprise

Last updated 12 November 2026For restaurant groups, chains, and franchise operators

Mise checks every supplier bill against what was ordered and what actually arrived, then shows your team the evidence. For groups running many stores, the enterprise program adds the controls your head office, finance, and IT teams ask for: single sign-on, per-store permissions, a readable audit trail, and Canada-first data handling.

Where this stands, plainly. Mise is in pilot-stage deployment. The controls below are marked IN PILOT when they run in the current pilot build, IN BUILD when they are designed and being implemented, and ROADMAP when they are committed scope with a later date. Nothing on this page is a certification claim. Our security posture and evidence process are detailed on the trust & security page.

Identity your IT team controls

  • Single sign-on (SSO) IN BUILD — SAML and OIDC sign-in through your identity provider, delivered by Clerk, our production identity provider. Your IdP stays the source of truth for who can sign in.
  • Enforced multi-factor authentication IN BUILD — MFA required by organization policy, including step-up proof for sensitive approvals.
  • Per-store roles IN PILOT — Owner, Manager, and Staff permissions are scoped store by store, with a founder-approved catalogue of 59 named permissions. Head-office roles do not silently become store access.
  • Joiner / mover / leaver handling IN BUILD — Invitations, role changes, and revocation are explicit recorded actions; revoking a person removes their sessions. SCIM provisioning and directory sync are ROADMAP for phase 2.

Control over money movement

  • Approval bound to the exact order IN PILOT — A named person approves the exact contents of a purchase order; any edit invalidates the approval. Approvals carry a dollar ceiling.
  • No silent external actions IN PILOT — Supplier sends and other outbound actions require human approval tied to the exact payload. Mise never auto-pays, auto-credits, or dispatches on a model's say-so.
  • Deterministic arithmetic IN PILOT — Quantities and money use versioned decimal math, never floating point and never LLM output, on stock, cost, and order paths.

Audit trail and evidence

  • Readable audit log IN PILOT — Grants, revocations, approvals, counts, uploads, and support actions are recorded with actor, store, and timestamp. The log is written for a reviewer to actually read, not just to exist.
  • Evidence on every number IN PILOT — Each flagged price or quantity traces to its source: the agreement clause, the receiving record, or the calculator trace. Estimates are always labelled as estimates.
  • Support without impersonation IN PILOT — Our support console cannot impersonate your users. Elevated support access is owner-approved or disclosed break-glass, and it is audit-logged.
  • Data export and offboarding IN BUILD — Account export, retention clocks, and a manual offboarding checklist are part of the tenant lifecycle, so leaving Mise never means losing your records without a process.

How an enterprise pilot runs

We start with one or two stores, not a chain-wide rollout. The pilot measures what Mise promises: trusted purchase orders, invoice variance caught, and manager minutes per day.
