Trust & security

Last updated 12 November 2026Security contact: hello@antigrav.si

Mise handles supplier invoices, purchase orders, counts, and store records for restaurants. This page describes the controls that protect that data today, the compliance program we are building toward, and — just as plainly — what is not finished yet. It is written for your security review, not as marketing copy.

No certification is claimed. Mise is in pilot-stage deployment. We operate a SOC 2-ready control program for Canada (controls designed and being evidenced); we do not hold a SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate, or any other attestation today. Anything on this page marked as planned is not yet a contractual commitment.

Compliance program

  • SOC 2-ready, Canada first — Controls are mapped to the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality) with evidence collected as the pilot runs. A Type I observation window follows production launch; no date is promised on this page.
  • PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 — Privacy design follows Canada's PIPEDA accountability principles and Quebec's Law 25 requirements for our initial Ontario/Quebec pilot boundary. Legal sufficiency is reviewed by qualified local counsel before production customer data is processed; that review is in progress, not complete.
  • Data-processing agreement — Available for pilot customers under NDA, covering roles, subprocessors, security measures, incident notice, and return/deletion on exit.
  • Vendor questionnaires — We answer SIG/CAIQ-style questionnaires against evidence and mark unbuilt controls as such. Send yours to hello@antigrav.si.

Access control

  • Identity — Production sign-in is delivered by Clerk, including SAML/OIDC single sign-on and enforced MFA for enterprise organizations (in implementation for the production cell).
  • Authorization — Every request resolves the person to an authorized tenant and store membership at the server boundary. Callers cannot choose their own tenant scope.
  • Least privilege internally — Support staff use a separate internal console with role boundaries (support cannot open finance views). There is no user impersonation; elevated access is owner-approved or disclosed break-glass and is audit-logged.
  • Sessions — Staff sessions use HttpOnly, expiring cookies. Revocation removes access and is a recorded event.

Data protection

  • Tenant isolation — Database row-level security separates tenants and stores. An automated tenant-isolation leak suite runs in CI; a cross-tenant read fails the build.
  • Encryption — Data is encrypted in transit over TLS and at rest by our cloud database and storage providers. Secrets live in Google Secret Manager, never in source code or chat.
  • Document handling — Supplier documents are quarantined on intake. Extracted values remain candidates until a named person reviews them; OCR output never posts an accounting entry or a payment by itself.
  • Backups and recovery — A local restore drill has proven data, tenant isolation, and receipt continuity survive a restore into a fresh PostgreSQL 17 cluster. Production point-in-time recovery and published RPO/RTO are set before production launch and are not claimed today.
  • Retention and deletion — Retention periods for access events are not yet adopted; they await qualified counsel's answers, and no deletion schedule runs meanwhile. Account export and offboarding processes are part of the tenant lifecycle design.

Engineering and operations

  • Change control — All code lands through reviewed pull requests with green CI (build, typecheck, lint, unit and browser tests, dependency-boundary checks). Failed browser runs retain Playwright traces and error context as build artifacts for diagnosis.
  • Deterministic money math — The valuation calculator is held to 100% branch coverage and a mutation-score floor, re-measured in CI. Quantities and money never use floating point, and no model output sits on the balance, stock, or payment path.
  • Dependency hygiene — Critical dependency advisories block the cold-checkout CI job. Residual advisories are tracked openly in the repository until patched releases exist.
  • Web hardening — Applications send frame, referrer, content-type, permissions, and HSTS headers, asserted by tests. A nonce-based script Content-Security-Policy is staged for the production cell and is not yet enforced.
  • Incident response — An incident and breach-assessment procedure with named responders is required before production operations and is being finalized with counsel. Until then, report anything security-related to hello@antigrav.si; we acknowledge security reports within two business days.

Responsible disclosure

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in Mise or on antigrav.si, email hello@antigrav.si with enough detail to reproduce it. Please do not access other customers' data, degrade the service, or disclose the issue publicly before we have had a reasonable chance to fix it. We do not run a paid bug bounty today.
