Mise for ChatGPT — Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026Privacy policy for the Mise plugin for ChatGPT

This policy covers the Mise plugin for ChatGPT (the “plugin”), a product of Antigravity Analytics Private Limited. It does not cover any other Mise or Antigravity product or website.

1. Who we are

Mise is a product of Antigravity Analytics Private Limited. This policy explains what data the plugin receives, how it is used, and how long it is kept.

2. What the plugin does

You give the plugin numbers in your ChatGPT conversation — for example, the lines of an order, a delivery, and a supplier bill — and it checks them and shows you the result in the same conversation.

3. What data the plugin receives

Only what is in a tool call: the item names, quantities, prices, fees, times, and currency code you provide for a check. The plugin has no account system. It never asks for — and you should never give it — a password, login, or API key of any kind (including any till or point-of-sale login), payment card or bank details, or government ID numbers or other personal documents.

4. How that data is used

The numbers are used for one thing only: to run the check you asked for and return the result. They are not used to advertise to you, not sold, not shared with suppliers or any other business, and not used to build a profile of you or your business.

5. Retention — stated plainly

The plugin’s server is stateless. It keeps no copy of the lines you submit: the data is processed in memory to produce the result and is not written to a database, a file, or an analytics system by the plugin. Server logs are kept free of request bodies, so the numbers you submit do not appear in logs.

Two honest limits to that statement:

  • Your conversation lives in ChatGPT: what ChatGPT keeps of your conversation is governed by OpenAI’s privacy policy and your ChatGPT settings, not ours. Do not paste anything into the conversation that you would not want handled under those terms.
  • Support messages: if you contact us for support and choose to include your numbers in that message, that message is kept like ordinary support mail so we can answer you.

6. Cookies and tracking

The plugin sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and contains no advertising or tracking technology.

7. Children

The plugin is a business tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new “last updated” date before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: write to hello@antigrav.si.
