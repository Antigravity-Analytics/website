How it works

The 10-minute morning routine.

Every morning at 08:15: what could run out today, what yesterday cost you, a two-minute stock count, one order to approve. Longer than ten minutes means something’s wrong.

4-step setup

No same-day setup stress.

You work fourteen-hour days. Setup happens in steps, at your pace — we do the heavy lifting.

STEP 01

Connect your POS

Connect Clover and set a spending limit for managers — any order over CA$200 waits for the owner’s tap.

STEP 02

Your recipes, as a starting point

Tell us the recipe each dish started with. We know cooks adjust on the line — Mise measures against your original, not a guess.

STEP 03

We map your stock for you

Send us your item list. Mise matches it to your recipes on its own — no weeks of typing things in.

STEP 04

Count when it suits you

No full count on day one. Enter your opening stock whenever suits you — that’s when the morning briefs start.
