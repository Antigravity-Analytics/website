Setup

Set up with you, in four steps.

You work long days, so setup happens in steps, at your pace. Your store enters its own data with our tooling, and we walk through it together until each step holds. Nothing is entered for you, and nothing starts until the numbers are yours.

STEP 01

Connect your POS

You giveYour authorisation, inside Clover on a call together: one tap grants Mise read only access to your account. Nobody hands over a login.
Mise doesConnects through that authorisation, read only, and sets your manager limit. Any order over CA$200 waits for the owner.
Your timeAbout 10 minutes.
Done whenYour sales show in Mise the same day.
STEP 02

Your recipes, as a starting point

You giveThe recipe each dish started with, as it stands today.
Mise doesLoads them as the measure. Cooks adjust on the line; Mise measures against your original, not a guess.
Your timeAbout 20 minutes for a standard menu.
Done whenEvery dish has a starting recipe on record.
STEP 03

Enter your stock, with us beside you

You giveYour item list, from your shelves and your invoices.
Mise doesProvides the tooling and stays on the call while you enter each item, so every item lands the way your kitchen names it.
Your timeOne sitting, about 30 to 45 minutes for most kitchens.
Done whenYour stock list matches what is on your shelves.
STEP 04

Count when it suits you

You giveA first ten item count, whenever it suits.
Mise doesStarts the morning briefs from your count. No full count on day one.
Your timeTwo minutes, in the walk-in.
Done whenYour first 08:15 brief arrives the next morning.

Have ready before step one

  • Your Clover account, so you can authorise read only access on the call.
  • One recent supplier invoice.
  • Your recipes, as they stand.
  • A time for a first count, any time in the first week.

All of it is gatherable in one shift.

Leaving

Leaving is as plain as joining.

Cancelling is exactly as easy as signing up. There is no retention maze and no call you have to sit through to leave. Fees are non-refundable, and that is stated in the contract before you sign.
Your records on exit are covered by the data rights clause you received in writing before your trial started.
Coming back is on the intro terms of the day you return, never a worse rung for having left. Founding terms are not promised on a return; they belong to the cohort that stayed.
Marketing emails end with one click or one reply. The words are the same in every footer: unsubscribe at any time.

For a call, or a question before you join, write to hello@antigrav.si (your name, your store, two times that suit you). Every desk below is read, and a message to the wrong desk is forwarded.
